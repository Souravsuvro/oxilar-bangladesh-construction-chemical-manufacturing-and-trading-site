Roof slurry method

1. Clean and SSD the slab. 2. Mix slurry. 3. Brush first coat. 4. Cross-brush second coat when touch-dry. 5. Protect from rain for 24 h. 6. Sand-cement plaster with 300 ml Super-100 per bag.
